# `--regulatory` writes no motif scores

A difference in two `Extra` fields of `MotifFeature` rows, and in the chromosome name of `--spdi`, under flags no measured dataset is run with; nothing is set aside in the concordance figures.

Ensembl VEP's `--regulatory` annotates every record against the regulatory features and transcription-factor binding motifs of its cache (`AnnotationSource/Cache/RegFeat.pm`, read from the `_reg.gz` files beside the transcript files). A record inside a regulatory feature gets a `RegulatoryFeature` row: `regulatory_region_variant`, with `regulatory_region_ablation` or `regulatory_region_amplification` for a structural variant covering the feature. A record inside a motif gets a `MotifFeature` row (`TF_binding_site_variant`, with `TFBS_ablation` or `TFBS_amplification` likewise) carrying `MOTIF_NAME`, `MOTIF_POS`, `HIGH_INF_POS`, `MOTIF_SCORE_CHANGE` and `TRANSCRIPTION_FACTORS`. A record overlapping no transcript keeps its `intergenic_variant` row beside them. `--everything` switches `--regulatory` on (`Config.pm` line 362).

vep-rs reads the regulatory features from a cache converted with `scripts/data/storable_to_json.pl`, which writes them to `regulatory/<chr>/` and marks `info.json` with `regulatory: true`. It writes the same rows and terms, and `BIOTYPE`, `MOTIF_NAME`, `MOTIF_POS`, `STRAND` and `TRANSCRIPTION_FACTORS` as Ensembl VEP does. It writes `HIGH_INF_POS` and `MOTIF_SCORE_CHANGE` empty on every motif row. Ensembl VEP computes both from the motif's position weight matrix (`OutputFactory.pm` lines 1930-1939, through `MotifFeatureVariationAllele.pm` lines 153 and 183); the converter keeps the matrix, and vep-rs does not score it yet. Cell-type activity, `--cell_type` and regulatory GFF sources are not read. A cache converted without the regulatory tree keeps the warnings-file line saying no regulatory rows are written.

On ClinVar 20260321 and gnomAD v4.1 chromosome 21 against Ensembl VEP 116.2, every `RegulatoryFeature` and `MotifFeature` row matches by key, 2,419 of 2,419 and 4,451 of 4,451, and every field above matches on each.

When the FASTA names a chromosome unlike the cache, a regulatory-only record's rows under `--spdi` name it as the cache does in Ensembl VEP and as the FASTA does here, `21:30234967:A:G` against `chr21:30234967:A:G`. The regulatory feature hands the variant its slice there (`AnnotationType/RegFeat.pm` line 126), a slice vep-rs never has.

The `GRCh38-everything` golden corpus compares the two engines under `--everything` with the mask class `regulatory_feature_rows`, which the test runner and `scripts/concordance/compare_vep_formats.py --masks` both apply. The reference's `RegulatoryFeature` and `MotifFeature` rows are set aside, and the `most_severe_consequence` of each JSON record that lost such a row is recomputed from the rows that remain. `--keep-only regulatory_feature_rows` compares those rows alone.
