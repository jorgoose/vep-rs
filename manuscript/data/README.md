# Published measurement data

Every number in the vep-rs concordance and throughput results re-derives from the fifteen CSV files
in this directory and the side files under `transcript_set_parity/`. Nothing in the published results
depends on data that is not here. The provenance records under
[`docs/concordance-provenance/`](../../docs/concordance-provenance/) pin each run these files draw on:
the vep-rs sweep (a concordance record and a wall-time record sharing one `sweep_id`, the latter also
carrying the fastVEP and Perl VEP wall-time cells it reuses), the GRCh37 structural-variant reference
regeneration, the breakend batch replay, the cache-parity audit and the GRCh37 cache-113-versus-115
comparison. Each record carries the binary digests, dataset-inventory digest and per-machine verdicts
of its run.

## Recomputing the published statistics

**F1.** Pool `perl_tuples`, `rust_tuples` and `intersection` for the suites you want and take
`2I/(A+B)`. For an adjusted value on the SNP and indel suites, subtract that suite's masked pair count
(the sum of its `masked_pairs` rows in `discordance_taxonomy.csv`) from both raw denominators; the two
structural-variant rows carry their post-mask denominators in their own `adj_*` columns. Do not mix the
file-summed columns with the `*_union` columns: they are two different bases and a suite's raw and
adjusted F1 must never sit on two of them.

**Per-class F1.** In `f1_by_consequence_class.csv` and its per-dataset companion, a term's
intersection is `vep_tuples - vep_only`, the engine's total is that intersection plus `engine_only`,
and F1 is `2I/(A+B)` over those two totals; `intersection`, `engine_tuples`, `precision`, `recall` and
`f1` are stored as well so the arithmetic can be checked column by column. Term sets overlap, so one
tuple contributes to several terms and the per-class column does not pool back into the aggregate.

**Wall time.** Each cell's published figure is the median of its 20 per-machine observations. Rows with
`run_index=median_of_20` are the aggregates, and their `notes` field carries `p5`, `p25`, `p75`, `p95`,
`min`, `max` and `p95_p5_ratio`. All 56 cells use one percentile estimator, the Weibull plotting
position (R type 6), which is `statistics.quantiles(sorted(values), n=20, method="exclusive")` in
Python: index 0 is P5 and index 18 is P95. Every stored aggregate recomputes from that cell's own 20
observations, so the estimator never has to be inferred.

**The `notes` column.** A `;`-separated list of `key=value` pairs and bare tokens, each a fact about the
measurement itself. Every `wall_times.csv` row carries `methodology=cross_instance_parallel_clone`,
`sweep_id`, `arch`, `condition` (`sites_only` or `with_gt`), `local_nvme=true`, `n_timed` and `suite_id`;
a per-machine row adds `clone_run_index`, and an aggregate row adds the dispersion keys above plus
`n_clones` and `warmup_run_discarded_per_clone=1`. The `sweep_id` is the join key to the provenance
records, and a row belongs to exactly one sweep. In `f1_observations.csv` the `verdict` column carries
`n_tier`, `methodology`, `sweep_id` and `binary_x86` (the x86 build's digest prefix; the `binary_md5` column
is the ARM build's), and on the two structural-variant rows the file-summed basis (`sv_aggregation`), the
ground-truth set (`sv_ground_truth`, with `sv_gt_cache` on the GRCh37 row) and the `<CNV:TR>` swap-pair
total (`cnvtr_swap_pairs_total`); which tree the binaries were built from is recorded by the provenance
record, not by a row. In `f1_observations_fastvep.csv` the column carries
`suite`, `sweep_id`, the fastVEP version and `arch_independent`, and on the two structural-variant rows
the ground-truth set (`sv_per_vcf_ground_truth`), the file-summed basis (`sv_aggregation`), the
comparator, the symbolic-allele normalisation and `absent_from_cache`. In `perl_bench.csv` it carries the
probe kind (`perl_peak_memory_probe`, `single_probe_run_not_a_walltime_measurement`), the suite id, the
VEP invocation (`fork16_buffer5000`, `sites_only_canonical` or `with_genotypes` with `samples`), the
memory unit and source, the sample interval, the input and output record counts, the kernel, the
anonymous-memory maximum (`anon_max_mib`) on the sites-only rows, and on the genotype-carrying row the
cgroup limit, `oom_kill`, `end_reason`, `completed`, the wall-time cap and the input digest prefix. No
token records which run of the tooling wrote a row: the sweep id and the provenance record it names are
that trace.

**Figures.** `python3 manuscript/figures/generate_figures.py --all` renders both figures from these
CSVs in four formats each and writes a digest manifest to `manuscript/figures/MANIFEST.txt`. Its
Python dependencies are pinned in `manuscript/requirements.txt`.

## What is not here

Per-engine annotation outputs and per-machine diff reports total hundreds of gigabytes and are not
distributed. The aggregates every published figure is computed from are the files above, so no
published number depends on access to them. One consequence: the per-class attribution of residual
discordance cannot be re-derived from this directory.

Per-machine identifiers in the provenance records, and in the `clone` column of
`fastvep_extra_tuples_by_transcript.csv` and `fastvep_zero_f1_shapes.csv`, are anonymous labels assigned
per architecture, `clone-arm64-NN` and `clone-x86_64-NN`, numbered in order of first appearance within a
record; the same machine carries the same label in every field of that record. Every measurement,
digest, median and percentile is as measured.
